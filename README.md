# 💳 Credit Card Transaction & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing credit card transactions and customer data using **Microsoft Power BI**. The interactive dashboards provide insights into revenue, transaction volume, customer demographics, spending behavior, and card usage patterns.

The project contains two dashboards:
- **Credit Card Transaction Report:** Analyzes transaction revenue, transaction amounts, interest earned, card categories, expenditure types, and quarterly performance.
- **Credit Card Customer Report:** Analyzes customer revenue, income, age groups, job types, education levels, marital status, and weekly revenue trends.

The dashboards help users explore financial performance and customer behavior through interactive visualizations and filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall credit card revenue and transaction performance.
- Understand customer spending patterns and demographics.
- Compare revenue across card categories and transaction methods.
- Identify revenue trends across quarters and weeks.
- Explore customer segments by age, income, occupation, and education.
- Build interactive dashboards to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — Measures and KPI calculations
- **CSV Files** — Data sources

## 📊 Dashboard 1: Credit Card Transaction Report

This dashboard provides an overview of transaction performance and revenue distribution.

### Key Metrics
- Total Revenue: ₹55M approximately
- Total Transactions: 656K
- Total Transaction Amount: ₹45M approximately
- Total Interest Earned: ₹8M approximately

### Key Visualizations
- Revenue and transaction volume by quarter
- Revenue by card category
- Revenue by transaction method: Swipe, Chip, and Online
- Revenue by expenditure type
- Revenue by education level
- Revenue by customer job type
- Interactive filters for week, card category, gender, and other segments

### Key Insights
- Blue-category cards contribute the largest share of revenue.
- Swipe transactions generate the highest revenue among the three transaction methods.
- The third quarter records the highest quarterly revenue in the displayed report.
- Bills and entertainment are among the leading expenditure categories.
- Business customers contribute the highest revenue among the displayed job categories.

## 👥 Dashboard 2: Credit Card Customer Report

This dashboard focuses on customer demographics, financial profiles, and revenue trends.

### Key Metrics
- Total Revenue: ₹55M approximately
- Total Income: ₹576M approximately
- Total Interest Earned: ₹8M approximately
- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSS): 3.19

### Key Visualizations
- Revenue by age group
- Weekly revenue trends
- Revenue by top five states
- Revenue by marital status
- Revenue by income group
- Revenue by dependent count
- Revenue by education level
- Customer revenue by occupation
- Interactive filters for week, card category, quarter, gender, and transaction method

### Key Insights
- Customers aged 40–50 contribute the highest revenue among the displayed age groups.
- Business customers contribute the highest revenue among the listed occupations.
- Customers in the high-income group contribute significantly to revenue.
- The weekly revenue chart helps compare trends for male and female customers.
- The dashboard enables customer segmentation based on demographic and financial attributes.

## 🚀 How to Use This Project

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Install Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Download the `.pbix` file and open it in Power BI Desktop.
4. If required, update the data source paths to the downloaded CSV files.
5. Refresh the data to load the datasets.
6. Use the filters and interactive visuals to explore transaction performance and customer behavior.
